9.​ Create a new service account with the name pvviewer. 

Grant this Service account access to list all PersistentVolumes in the cluster 

by creating an appropriate cluster role called pvviewer-role and 

ClusterRoleBinding called pvviewer-role-binding.



> kubectl get serviceaccount pvviewer -n lab
NAME       SECRETS   AGE
pvviewer   0         97s

> kubectl get clusterrole pvviewer-role
NAME            CREATED AT
pvviewer-role   2025-02-15T16:03:18Z

> kubectl get clusterrolebinding pvviewer-role-binding
NAME                    ROLE                        AGE
pvviewer-role-binding   ClusterRole/pvviewer-role   30s
